Before You Cross

What you need to enter Canada

A regular U.S. driver's license is not enough to cross into Canada.

Accepted Documents

You need one of the following:

  • ✓ Valid U.S. Passport
  • ✓ U.S. Passport Card
  • ✓ Enhanced Driver's License (where available)
  • ✓ NEXUS Card

Age Requirement

Ontario's legal drinking age is 19. You must be at least 19 to access nightlife venues. All travelers must be 18+ to book.

Border Rules

  • ✗ No cannabis across the border — federal offense
  • ⚠ Criminal record issues, including DUI, may affect entry
  • ✓ Be honest with border officers
  • ✓ Declare all purchases on return

Safe & Organized

  • ✓ Emergency support contact
  • ✓ Venue access rules
  • ✓ Border document reminders
  • ✓ Conduct expectations
